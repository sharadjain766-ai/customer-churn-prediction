## 📌 Project Overview

**Customer Churn Prediction** is a Machine Learning application designed to identify customers who are likely to **churn (leave a service)**.

The system analyzes customer information such as:

- Customer tenure
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Contract type
- Internet service
- Payment method
- Customer support services
- Demographic and subscription details

The trained model processes these features and predicts whether the customer is likely to **Churn** or **Not Churn**.

The application also provides a **churn probability** to give an estimate of the customer's risk level.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to help businesses identify customers who may be at risk of leaving their service.

Early identification of potential churn can help businesses:

- Improve customer retention
- Identify high-risk customers
- Take preventive actions
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce customer loss
